Type: grilling
Status: resolved

## Comments

- V1 is Vietnamese-first. Phone and Zalo are required contact channels; WhatsApp is optional and must not block launch. This does not decide whether English remains required, becomes secondary, or moves to a later milestone.

## Answer

The V1 launch-input inventory is resolved. Resolution means every reviewed input is classified with explicit approval boundaries; it does **not** mean the currently missing launch content is approved.

### Approval model

Use two explicit roles even if one person later holds both:

- **Product / website approver**: approves public brand direction, copy, CTA language, content assembly, review display, and evidence of media publication rights.
- **Operational approver**: confirms price, itinerary, inclusions/exclusions, timing, capacity, pickup, safety, weather, child-participation, and other rules against the real tour.

Operational facts require operational approval before the product layer words them. Named or depicted family members approve their own public identification; identifiable guests require explicit promotional-use permission. The individuals holding both approval roles remain missing and required before launch-content sign-off.

Approval must be recorded in project artifacts and must not be inferred from an old banner, previous AI copy, an existing media file, or prior social publication. Each approved fact should record its value, approver role, evidence/source, and a date or note when useful. Media later needs a small inventory of filename/identifier, subject, original location, publication status, identifiable people, consent, and restrictions.
