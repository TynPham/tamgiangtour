# Choose the V1 editable content boundary

Type: grilling
Status: resolved
Blocked by: 01, 02

## Question

Given the fixed Markdown/MDX direction and no-CMS constraint, which V1 content belongs in localized Markdown/MDX, which small structured facts require a typed source, and which data belongs in Supabase only because it is generated by visitors?

Choose the smallest boundary that keeps price, tour facts, FAQs, reviews, contact destinations, and translations consistent without designing a general content platform.

## Answer

### Localized narrative and page content

- Put page-level editorial copy in locale-specific Markdown/MDX. This includes positioning, tour introduction, experience descriptions, family/local story, policy explanations, and longer SEO/editorial content.
- Use one localized content file per published page. Associate localized equivalents through Ticket 02's stable internal content key.
- Keep the localized title, slug, summary, and SEO description with the localized page record.
- Prefer plain Markdown. Use MDX only for a small approved set of presentational components when prose genuinely needs them; do not turn MDX into a general component or configuration system.
- Do not embed shared structured facts such as price, phone, coordinates, itinerary times, inclusion flags, or other locale-independent operational facts in narrative content.
- Do not place reusable CTA labels, validation messages, navigation labels, or shared interface copy in page content. Those remain in centralized locale dictionaries.
- Do not create placeholder English content files. Add an English page only when that page's required English content is complete and approved.

### Shared structured tour facts

- Keep one repository-owned typed TypeScript record for the single V1 tour. Do not use Supabase or another database as the editable source of truth for operator-authored V1 tour facts.
- The record may contain locale-independent values and stable semantic keys such as the stable tour key, canonical VND pricing facts, duration and operating-time facts, ordered itinerary item keys and times, inclusion/exclusion keys, conditional flags, approved suitability/safety/policy values needed programmatically, and references to canonical contact and meeting-point records.
- Keep localized tour names, labels, explanations, narrative copy, and localized SEO/editorial text outside this record.
- Localized content references structured facts through stable semantic keys rather than duplicating canonical values.
- Preserve explicit states such as approved, conditional, unavailable, or unresolved only where needed to control publishability or prevent an unapproved operational promise. Do not introduce a generic workflow or state machine.
- Do not introduce generic schemas, repository abstractions, plugin systems, database-backed content editing, CMS behavior, or multi-tour architecture.

### Public contact and meeting-point destinations

- Keep one repository-owned typed canonical record for the approved phone number, Zalo destination and publishability state, Google Maps URL, Maps place label, coordinates, and approved human-readable address, parking/travel facts, and related location data when available.
- Components and localized content reference these canonical fields. Do not duplicate literal phone numbers, URLs, coordinates, or other canonical destination values across components or Markdown.
- Localized CTA labels and explanatory directions remain in locale dictionaries or localized page content.
- Omit unapproved contact channels or location facts from public output; do not substitute placeholders. WhatsApp remains absent unless explicitly approved later.
- Keep Ticket 03's operator-notification destination separate from public contact content. Once confirmed, it belongs in deployment/integration configuration and must not be exposed in public Markdown or the public contact/location record.
- Do not introduce contact or location tables, Supabase-backed editable contact content, or multi-location abstractions.

### Pricing facts

- Keep canonical pricing in the typed V1 tour record as structured VND values, not preformatted localized strings.
- Represent only approved pricing facts required by the real V1 offer: amount, pricing unit, minimum charge or group rule, child pricing when applicable, and mandatory surcharges when applicable.
- Preserve unresolved state until required pricing facts are operationally approved. If required pricing is incomplete or unapproved, block publication of the affected V1 offer rather than display placeholder, guessed, or partial pricing.
- Format VND at the localized presentation boundary and keep localized pricing labels outside canonical pricing data.
- Explanatory pricing prose may live in localized Markdown/MDX and reference canonical structured values.
- V1 does not introduce foreign-currency storage, exchange-rate behavior, discount rules, promotion logic, a pricing engine, or dynamic pricing.

### Itinerary, inclusions, policies, and safety

- Keep promise-affecting operational facts in the typed V1 tour record: itinerary item key, order, approved time, activity state (`guaranteed`, `conditional`, `illustrative`, or `unavailable`), inclusion/exclusion keys and conditionality, approved policy values affecting enquiry handling, and approved suitability/safety rules needed for display or validation.
- Keep localized activity names, descriptions, caveats, policy explanations, and safety guidance in locale-specific content linked through the same stable semantic keys.
- Required unresolved operational facts block publication of the affected offer or section. Optional or unapproved items are omitted.
- Narrative copy or imagery must not turn an unapproved or illustrative item into a guest-facing promise.
- Conditional items retain their condition in structured data so every presentation communicates the same constraint consistently.
- Do not encode successful fishing outcomes, visible sunset, a Ru Cha visit, lagoon-hut access, specific seafood, or SUP participation as guaranteed unless operationally approved.
- Do not introduce a rules engine, policy engine, itinerary builder, or generalized activity model.

### FAQ content

- Keep FAQ questions and answers in locale-specific Markdown/MDX. Give each FAQ item a stable semantic key and explicit display order.
- Vietnamese FAQ content determines V1 launch readiness. Do not create incomplete English counterparts.
- When an answer refers to price, timing, inclusions, safety, pickup, or policy facts, reference canonical structured values rather than duplicating conflicting literals.
- Required FAQ items do not publish until their underlying operational facts and Vietnamese wording are approved. Optional unanswered questions are omitted cleanly.
- Keep reusable FAQ interface labels in centralized locale dictionaries, separate from FAQ editorial content.
- Do not introduce Supabase-backed FAQ storage, per-item database records, CMS editing, FAQ categories, FAQ search infrastructure, or a generalized FAQ system.

### Reviews and social proof

- Keep curated V1 review/social-proof records in a repository-owned typed source, not Markdown page prose or Supabase.
- Each publishable record contains only the traceability fields it needs, such as a stable review key, public source/provider, source URL or approved private-feedback evidence, approved reviewer attribution, source language, verified rating and date when available, approved excerpt or summary, and publication/permission state.
- Store aggregate rating and review count only as a verified, source-attributed snapshot with its source and capture date. Do not invent or retain stale unattributed numbers.
- Localized translations or summaries are separate approval-gated presentation fields linked through the stable review key. They must not overwrite, distort, or misrepresent source wording.
- With no verified content, keep the typed structure empty. The section may remain hidden or intentionally empty during development; do not fabricate testimonials, ratings, counts, identities, or photos.
- At least one verified attributable source of real social proof remains required for V1 launch.
- Do not introduce Supabase-backed review storage, website review submissions, review scraping, moderation workflows, review-management tooling, or CMS behavior.

### Approval and publishability

- Use small repository-owned publication metadata only.
- Localized pages and FAQ collections may carry a stable content key, locale, completeness/publication state, and an approval evidence note sufficient to trace product/content approval.
- Operational structured facts retain only the meaningful state needed for publication safety, such as `approved`, `conditional`, `unavailable`, or `unresolved`, plus enough evidence to trace operational approval.
- Review records retain their own source, permission, and publication evidence.
- Media publishability is determined by the separate asset inventory, which owns source, permission/consent, and restrictions. Content records reference approved asset keys and do not duplicate rights metadata.
- Public builds expose only complete, approved localized pages and approved operational facts/assets.
- Draft, incomplete, or unapproved English content does not appear in public routes or indexing.
- Required unresolved Vietnamese content blocks the affected page. Optional unapproved sections are omitted cleanly.
- Do not introduce editorial roles, approval queues, scheduled publishing, revision-history systems, database approval workflows, or CMS preview infrastructure.

### Visitor-generated enquiry data and Supabase

- Supabase PostgreSQL stores only visitor-generated V1 `Booking enquiry` data and Ticket 03's required operational metadata: internal record ID, idempotency key, validated submitted fields, normalized locale and source page, server-generated creation timestamp, an optional Ticket 06-defined attribution snapshot or correlation identifier, and notification-delivery diagnostics needed for observable recovery.
- Supabase is not the editable source of truth for tour facts, pricing, public contact information, localized pages, FAQ, reviews/social proof, policies, or safety content.
- Do not introduce V1 tables for tours, customer accounts, availability, slots, payments, booking status, review submissions, or content management.
- Keep abuse-control signals separate from enquiry/customer and acquisition data and retain only the minimum needed for abuse protection.
- The operator-notification destination remains deployment/integration configuration, not part of the enquiry record or public content.
- Exact columns, constraints, indexes, retention mechanics, and migrations belong in the later implementation specification.
- Use the Supabase client and explicit migrations; do not introduce an ORM.
- Supabase Storage may host approved media assets if implementation needs it, but it is not the editorial source of truth. The repository asset inventory remains authoritative for rights, consent, restrictions, and publishability.

### V1 content boundary summary

1. **Localized Markdown/MDX** owns page-level editorial prose, localized page metadata, policy explanations, and localized FAQ content. Centralized locale dictionaries own reusable interface strings.
2. **Repository-owned typed canonical data** owns the single tour's locale-independent facts, public contact/location destinations, structured VND pricing, promise-affecting operational states, curated traceable reviews/social proof, approval metadata, and references to approved media assets.
3. **Supabase visitor-generated data** owns durable `Booking enquiry` records and only the minimal operational metadata required by Ticket 03, with optional acquisition context bounded by Ticket 06.

### Unresolved dependencies

- Ticket 01's missing launch inputs still require factual, operational, content, review, and media approval before the affected Vietnamese pages can publish.
- The repository asset inventory must be completed before media asset keys are publishable.
- Ticket 03's operator-notification destination and recovery mechanism remain unresolved launch dependencies outside public content.
- Ticket 06 must define optional attribution fields, privacy/consent, precedence, and retention before acquisition context is attached to enquiries.
- The later implementation specification owns exact TypeScript shapes, content paths/loaders, Supabase schema details, migrations, and validation mechanics within this boundary.

## Comments

- Resolved a three-part V1 content boundary: localized Markdown/MDX for editorial copy, small repository-owned typed sources for canonical facts and curated evidence-backed content, and Supabase only for visitor-generated enquiries and their minimal operational metadata.
