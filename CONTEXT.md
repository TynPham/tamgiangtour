# Tam Giang Tour

Shared business language for presenting the family-operated Tam Giang Lagoon experience and handling guest interest.

## Language

**Booking enquiry**:
A guest's request to take the tour. It does not reserve availability, confirm the trip, represent accepted payment, or create a booking code.
_Avoid_: Booking, reservation, confirmed booking

**Confirmed booking**:
A booking enquiry that the family has reviewed and explicitly accepted through direct follow-up with the guest.
_Avoid_: Form confirmation, submission confirmation
